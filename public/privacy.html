Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

"MoTrail Sync" is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner to back up the owner's own cycling activity files (GPX / FIT) to the owner's own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

The app uses the Google Drive API, authorized by the owner, to upload the owner's activity files into a folder in the owner's own Google Drive.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, store, sell, or share data about any other person. It has no other users. Google account data is not transferred to any third party.

Data storage

Authorization tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository settings.
